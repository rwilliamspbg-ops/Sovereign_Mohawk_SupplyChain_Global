# GENESIS_MANIFESTO

Sovereign Mohawk Supply Chain Global is a commitment to sovereignty-first trade intelligence.

## Principles

- Sovereignty first: no raw logistics data leaves local custody by default.
- Collaboration without centralization: global model quality from local commercial intelligence.
- Verifiable trust: attestation, proofs, and policy signatures are mandatory.
- Compliance by design: governance and data controls are built into the runtime.
- Resilience for all: from small suppliers to global ports and 3PL networks.
